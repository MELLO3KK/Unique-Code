Why it's not a QR code: QR codes have three big square "finder patterns" in the corners and use Reed–Solomon on a different grid. A Data Matrix (ISO/IEC 16022, ECC200) instead has an L-shaped solid bar on two edges and a dashed "clock track" on the other two — more compact, just as scannable, and loved by factory marking systems. Point your phone camera at the generated image (or paste the page URL into another device) to prove it decodes. The server also decodes every image it renders and shows the check result below.
