Rules of the platform
  1. Only verified business brand accounts can generate product codes.
  2. A code can only be generated by the brand that owns it — never by customers.
  3. Customers scan the code on the homepage to prove they bought the product.
  4. All records are saved permanently in the brand's ledger.
