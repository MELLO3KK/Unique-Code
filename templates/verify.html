Verify your product

The code image attached to your product already contains its unique serial — scanning it fills the first field automatically.

A counterfeit can copy the printed image, but it cannot know the secret passcode stored only on the brand's verification server. Wrong pairing → the product is fake.

How this works

  1. The brand creates an account and registers each product unit — one unique serial + one secret passcode per item.
  2. The serial is encoded in the scannable code image attached to the product; the passcode ships privately with it.
  3. You scan the code, land here, and enter the passcode.
  4. AUTHENTIC: serial + passcode match. FAKE: they don't. Already verified elsewhere? You'll get a clone/resale warning.
