How Proven works
  1. A business brand creates its account on the dashboard — only brands can generate product codes.
  2. The brand prints the code + a secret passcode on the packaging.
  3. Customers buy the product, scan the code and enter the passcode.
  4. Every proof of purchase is saved forever in the brand's record book.
